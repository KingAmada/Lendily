Terms and Conditions

By sending a borrow request, you confirm that the request details, lender ID, bank details, repayment date, interest or markup, and purpose are true and complete.

You understand that Lendily records the request as evidence of the proposed agreement. The lender may approve, reject, or counter the request before it becomes active.

Privacy Statement

Lendily uses your profile, bank details, request details, and liveness verification status to process and protect the borrow request.

Your information is shown only where needed for the lender to review the request, create receipts, verify repayment, and keep an agreement history.

Do not submit another person's identity, bank account, or face verification. You remain responsible for the accuracy of the information you provide.

Lendily is a recordkeeping and agreement workflow tool. It does not guarantee repayment, credit approval, fund recovery, legal enforcement, or lender profit.
